Jot

A minimal CLI tool to store your thoughts and notes.

Jot is a fast, simple and reliable command line tool written in Nim to help you capture, organize and retrieve your notes effortlessly from the terminal. Your notes are stored locally in a SQLite database. No cloud. No tracking. Just your notes, completely private.

CLI Tool Personal Project Active

About Jot

Jot is built for those moments when ideas strike or when you just need a place to dump your thoughts. It stays out of your way and lets you focus on what matters — your thoughts.

  • Add, list, view, search and delete notes
  • Automatically organized by date
  • Search notes by keyword
  • Mark important notes with a star
  • SQLite for speed and reliability
  • Works everywhere the terminal works

Tech Stack

Nim SQLite Nimble Terminal

Installation

Install Jot using nimble:

1nimble install jot

Usage

Quick examples to get you started.

Run jot --help to see all available commands.
