About me

Developer, system thinker, calm under pressure.

I started writing HTML on a borrowed laptop and never stopped. Today I lead fullstack teams that ship multi-tenant SaaS platforms used by hundreds of thousands of people — and I still review every pull request that touches the design system.

My sweet spot is the messy middle: turning a fuzzy product idea into a typed, tested, observable codebase. I care about performance budgets, accessible markup, honest documentation and interfaces that feel instant. When something breaks at 2 a.m., I'd rather fix the root cause than patch the symptom.

  • Based in Konoha — working remote across EST / CET / IST time zones.
  • Mentor — ran 30+ code reviews and 12 workshops for junior developers.
  • Response time — replies to every message within one business day.
  • Also — API archaeology, typography, and an unreasonable love of shortcuts.
